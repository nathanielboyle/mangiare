How Romans eat: notes from the guide
  • At a trattoria you don't need every course, but order two or three per person (an antipasto, a secondo and a contorno, or a primo then a secondo) so the kitchen isn't firing single plates.
  • Pizza tonda is thin, crisp and chewy with barely any rim. Forni sell pizza bianca and rossa by weight; try bianca stuffed with mortadella.
  • Street food is eaten standing, leaning or perched for a minute before moving on.
  • At markets, say "buongiorno" when you walk up and "grazie, arrivederci" when you leave. Ask "posso?" before touching produce, and use one bag per product.
  • Gelato has less air and less fat than ice cream and is served slightly warmer. Look for seasonal fruit sorbetti.
